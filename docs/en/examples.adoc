== Sample messages

The samples are part of the specification package `{schema-uri}.zip` (directory `samples`).

=== restriction.xml – roadworks, alternating one-lane traffic

The publication contains a partial closure of road I/10 in Desná (Jablonec nad Nisou district) due to
construction works with alternating traffic in one lane.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/restriction.xml[tags=**]
----

Envelope and header::
Root `d2:payload` of type `sit:SituationPublication` with the `profileName` and `profileVersion` attributes
identifying the format, `publicationTime` and `publicationCreator`. `feedType` refers to the data source.

Situation::
The situation contains two records in version 6. A record of type `ConstructionWorks` describes the cause
(construction works, `constructionWork`), a record of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` the impact
(alternating traffic in one lane, `singleAlternateLineTraffic`, mandatory – `complianceOption` =
`mandatory`). The value `probabilityOfOccurrence` = `certain` – the closure is already in force at the time
of publication.

Validity::
Both records are valid from 30{nbsp}August 2023 7:00 to 1{nbsp}September 2023 23:59.

Location::
The location is described by all methods: OpenLR, ALERT-C, chainage of road 10 (106 797–107 507 m),
Global Network sections of version 22.12 (five sections in travel order, 126853–2901326) and the location
path `gmlLineString` in WGS-84.

Other content::
`source` gives the originator of the information (ŘSD), `generalPublicComment` the text of the closure
decision and `urlLink` a link to details.

=== closure-junction.xml – closure of a junction with three arms

Full closure of the junction of roads II/259 and III/25924 in Vrátno (Mladá Boleslav district) due to sewer
construction. The sections meet in one node and do not form a contiguous chain, so the location is a group of
sections.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/closure-junction.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
One record of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` in version 5: road closure (`roadClosed`) in both
directions with remaining capacity 0, mandatory (`complianceOption` = `mandatory`).

Validity::
From 7{nbsp}October 2026 10:01 to 31{nbsp}October 2026 23:59.

Location::
`LocationGroupByList` with three linear locations, each on one road: road II/259 (two Global Network sections
in travel order), an unnumbered connecting link (one section) and road III/25924 (one section). Each location
carries its own location path `gmlLineString`; the sections serve both directions
(`directionRelativeOnLinearSection` = `both`).

=== closure-route.xml – closure along two adjoining roads

Full closure of roads III/34041 and III/34042 in Úhřetice (Chrudim district) due to road surface repair. The
location runs contiguously from one road to the other, so it is a route of two parts.

[source,xml]
----
include::{samplesdir}/closure-route.xml[tags=**]
----

Situation::
One record of type `RoadOrCarriagewayOrLaneManagement` in version 4: road closure (`roadClosed`) in both
directions with remaining capacity 0, mandatory.

Validity::
From 21{nbsp}September 2026 7:00 to 1{nbsp}October 2026 23:59.

Location::
`ItineraryByIndexedLocations` with two parts in travel order: part 1 on road III/34041 (four Global Network
sections), part 2 on the adjoining road III/34042 (four sections). Each part is a linear location
`SingleRoadLinearLocation` with its own location path `gmlLineString`; the end of the first part is the start of
the second. The sections serve both directions (`directionRelativeOnLinearSection` = `both`).
